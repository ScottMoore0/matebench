# Corpora: provenance and terms

No third-party corpus is vendored in this repository. Each is fetched or
generated by `fetch_corpora.py`, and this file records where it comes from,
what it may be used for, and what it was already used for - the last point
matters, because an engine tuned on a corpus cannot be fairly scored on it.

## The held-out rule

Any public corpus will be tuned on. The reference results already show what
that does: on matetrack MateHunter and Huntsman were "indistinguishable" for a
week; on ChestUCI, which neither was tuned on, they are separated by depth in
opposite directions at p = 0.001 and p = 0.0005. **Scoring for a leaderboard
must use a rotating held-out set** that submitters do not see in advance; the
public corpora are for development. The generated set is the natural seed for
that, because its terms permit anything and its generator is in the MateProver
repository.

## Ground truth by construction

Where a corpus carries a proof - ChestUCI's `bm #N`, or a MateProver
certificate - a claim of exactly that depth is verified by the corpus itself,
and the prover check is redundant. Where it carries only a stipulation, every
claim is verified. The harness records which applied.
