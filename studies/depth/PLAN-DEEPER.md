## What would count

on mate in 10 and 11 together. On ChestUCI at mate in 10 to 13, MateBench measured
MateHunter +478/-59 (`rounds/round-1/CLOSE.md`).

- A balance favouring MateHunter there, with p < 0.05, supports a gain that

  10 and 11. It cannot exclude an effect that begins at mate in 12 or deeper.
