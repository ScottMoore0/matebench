## Protocol, fixed now

- **Track:** finding, with speed on positions both arms verified. Minimality and
  absence are not run: no entrant emits the certificates those tracks now
  require (docs/CERTIFICATES.md).
- **Runner:** `bench/submit.py`, one thread per engine, verification by
  MateProver 0.2.0 at 1,000,000 nodes per claim.
- **Budgets:** each comparison is run three times, at **10,000,000**, **100,000**
  and **10,000** nodes per position. 10,000,000 is the reference budget. The
  pool is shallow, so at that budget every arm may solve nearly everything and
  leave no discordant pairs; the two smaller budgets are fixed here, before any
  run, so that choosing a budget cannot follow the results.
- **Comparisons:** MateHunter 19 against Stockfish 19; MateHunter 19 against
  Huntsman 1; Huntsman 1 against Stockfish 19.
- **Result:** the discordant pairs on verified claims per band, with a two-sided
  sign test, as TRACKS.md specifies. A comparison with no discordant pairs is
  reported as not established, never as a tie.
- **Development split:** the same comparisons at the same budgets on the 3,962
  development positions, as the out-of-sample re-measurement of MateHunter that
  `results/reference/REFERENCE.md` calls for. Run after the held-out set.
